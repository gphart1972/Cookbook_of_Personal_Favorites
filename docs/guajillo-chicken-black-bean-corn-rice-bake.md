# Guajillo Chicken, Black Bean & Corn Rice Bake

> **Takeaway:** A simple one-pan dinner with guajillo-seasoned chicken, rice, black beans, and corn baked together so the seasoned chicken flavors the rice as everything cooks.

## Overview

This recipe started as a simple kitchen experiment and came out perfectly.

Preseasoned **Pollo con Guajillo** chicken is baked directly with uncooked rice, black beans, and corn. The dish stays covered while baking, allowing the rice to absorb the water along with the seasoned juices from the chicken.

It is an easy, filling meal with very little preparation and only one baking dish to clean.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 package preseasoned **Pollo con Guajillo** chicken
- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 1 can corn, drained and rinsed
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 3 cups water
- Butter, enough to grease a 9 x 13-inch baking dish

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Baking Dish

Preheat the oven to:

```text
325°F (163°C)
```

Butter the inside of a **9 x 13-inch baking dish**.

### 2. Mix the Rice, Beans, and Corn

In a large mixing bowl, combine:

- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 1 can corn, drained and rinsed
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed

Mix until evenly combined.

### 3. Add the Chicken

Place the preseasoned **Pollo con Guajillo** chicken in the buttered baking dish.

### 4. Add the Rice Mixture

Add the rice, corn, and black bean mixture to the baking dish around and over the chicken, spreading it evenly through the pan.

### 5. Add the Water

Pour:

```text
3 cups water
```

evenly into the baking dish.

### 6. Cover and Bake

Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil.

Bake at:

```text
325°F (163°C)
```

for:

```text
1 hour
```

The rice should be tender and the liquid absorbed. The chicken should be fully cooked and reach an internal temperature of **165°F (74°C)**.

---

## Kitchen Notes

- The tested ratio is **2 cups uncooked rice to 3 cups water**.
- Keep the dish covered while baking so the rice can steam and absorb the liquid properly.
- The guajillo seasoning and juices from the chicken flavor the rice, beans, and corn as they cook.
- No additional seasoning was needed in the original version.

> **Tested result:** The rice came out fully cooked and the overall dish was considered a keeper.
