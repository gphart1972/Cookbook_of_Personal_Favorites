
# Baked Rice and Ground Chuck

A hearty, savory baked rice dinner combining golden mushroom soup, French onion soup, black beans, and browned ground chuck. Finished with salsa, sour cream, or both.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups dry long-grain white rice
- 1 can Golden Mushroom soup
- 1 can French Onion soup
- 1 can black beans
- 1 lb ground chuck
- 1/2 soup can of water
- Salsa, for serving (optional)
- Sour cream, for serving (optional)

## Equipment

- 9 × 13-inch baking dish
- Large mixing bowl
- Large skillet with lid
- Mixing spoon

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Rice Mixture

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Pour the Golden Mushroom soup and French Onion soup into a large mixing bowl.
3. Add approximately 1/2 soup can of water.
4. Stir until the soups and water are thoroughly combined.
5. Add the black beans and mix well.
6. Add the dry long-grain white rice.
7. Stir thoroughly to distribute the rice evenly throughout the mixture.

### 2. Bake the Rice

1. Pour the rice mixture into a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
2. Spread evenly across the dish.
3. Cover tightly with aluminum foil to retain moisture.
4. Bake for approximately 90 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed.

### 3. Brown the Ground Chuck

1. While the rice is baking, place the ground chuck in a large skillet.
2. Brown the meat over medium heat, breaking it into small pieces as it cooks.
3. Cook until thoroughly browned and the internal temperature reaches 160°F.
4. **Do not drain the grease.** Reserve all rendered fat and cooking juices in the skillet.
5. Keep the cooked ground chuck covered until ready to combine.

### 4. Combine the Rice and Beef

1. Remove the finished rice from the oven.
2. Transfer the baked rice mixture back into the large mixing bowl.
3. Add the browned ground chuck.
4. Pour all reserved grease and cooking juices from the skillet into the bowl.
5. Stir thoroughly until the meat, rice, beans, and cooking juices are evenly combined.

### 5. Serve

1. Spoon the finished mixture into individual bowls.
2. Top with your choice of:
   - Salsa
   - Sour cream
   - Both salsa and sour cream
3. Serve warm.

## Cook's Notes

- **Ground Chuck Grease:** Keeping the rendered fat is intentional. It adds richness and carries the beef flavor throughout the rice.
- **Soup Base:** Golden Mushroom and French Onion soups provide the savory foundation of the dish.
- **Black Beans:** Drain the beans if a firmer rice texture is preferred. Using the bean liquid may produce a softer mixture.
- **Rice Texture:** Keep the baking dish tightly covered to prevent moisture loss. If the rice is still firm, add a little hot water and continue baking until tender.
- **Serving:** Salsa adds acidity and brightness, while sour cream provides a cool, creamy contrast.

## Variations

- **Spicy:** Add diced jalapeños or hot salsa.
- **Cheesy:** Sprinkle shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack over individual servings.
- **Tex-Mex:** Add cumin, chili powder, or diced green chiles.
- **Extra Hearty:** Increase the ground chuck to 1 1/2 pounds.
