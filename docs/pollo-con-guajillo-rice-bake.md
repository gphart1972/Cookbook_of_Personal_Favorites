# 🌶️🍗 𝗣𝗢𝗟𝗟𝗢 𝗖𝗢𝗡 𝗚𝗨𝗔𝗝𝗜𝗟𝗟𝗢 𝗥𝗜𝗖𝗘 𝗕𝗔𝗞𝗘 🍚

This one started as one of those:

“Let’s put all of this in a baking dish and see what happens.”

And sometimes those experiments become recipes worth keeping. 😁

Pre-seasoned **pollo con guajillo** gives the chicken plenty of flavor, while the rice cooks underneath it with black beans and corn and soaks up everything in the pan.

Best part?

There’s no need to cook the rice first.

Mix it, cover it, put it in the oven, and leave it alone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 🥘 𝗪𝗛𝗔𝗧 𝗬𝗢𝗨'𝗟𝗟 𝗡𝗘𝗘𝗗

### 🍗 𝗖𝗵𝗶𝗰𝗸𝗲𝗻

- 1 package pre-seasoned pollo con guajillo chicken

### 🍚 𝗥𝗶𝗰𝗲 𝗕𝗮𝘀𝗲

- 2 cups uncooked rice
- 1 can corn, drained and rinsed
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 3 cups water

### 🧈 𝗙𝗼𝗿 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗣𝗮𝗻

- Butter, enough to coat a 9×13-inch baking dish

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 1️⃣ 𝗣𝗿𝗲𝗽 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗽𝗮𝗻

Heat the oven to **325°F**.

Butter the inside of a 9×13-inch baking dish.

Don’t just hit it with cooking spray.

The butter adds a little extra richness to the rice and helps keep everything from sticking.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 2️⃣ 𝗔𝗱𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗰𝗵𝗶𝗰𝗸𝗲𝗻

Place the pollo con guajillo chicken in the bottom of the baking dish.

No need to precook it.

The chicken will cook along with the rice and vegetables.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 3️⃣ 𝗠𝗶𝘅 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗿𝗶𝗰𝗲

In a bowl, combine:

- 🍚 2 cups uncooked rice
- 🌽 Drained and rinsed corn
- 🫘 Drained and rinsed black beans

Mix everything together, then spread it around the chicken in the baking dish.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 4️⃣ 𝗔𝗱𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝘄𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗿

Pour **3 cups of water** evenly over the rice mixture.

Make sure most of the rice is down in the liquid rather than sitting dry on top.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 5️⃣ 𝗖𝗼𝘃𝗲𝗿 𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗯𝗮𝗸𝗲

Cover the baking dish tightly with aluminum foil.

Bake at **325°F for 1 hour**.

And this is one of those recipes where you really do want to leave the foil alone.

The trapped steam is what cooks the rice.

### ⚠️ 𝗗𝗢𝗡'𝗧 𝗞𝗘𝗘𝗣 𝗢𝗣𝗘𝗡𝗜𝗡𝗚 𝗧𝗛𝗘 𝗢𝗩𝗘𝗡 𝗔𝗡𝗗 𝗣𝗘𝗘𝗞𝗜𝗡𝗚.

Yes, I know.

We all want to peek. 😂

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 🍽️ 𝗧𝗛𝗘 𝗥𝗘𝗦𝗨𝗟𝗧

After an hour, the rice should be tender, the liquid absorbed, and the chicken cooked through.

The corn gives it a little sweetness, the black beans make it more substantial, and the guajillo seasoning from the chicken works its way into the whole dish while it cooks.

This one came out exactly the way I wanted it the first time I made it.

Simple ingredients.

One baking dish.

Very little cleanup.

And most importantly:

**𝗜𝘁 𝗰𝗮𝗺𝗲 𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝗴𝗼𝗼𝗱 𝗲𝗻𝗼𝘂𝗴𝗵 𝘁𝗼 𝗺𝗮𝗸𝗲 𝗮𝗴𝗮𝗶𝗻.**

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

## 🌶️ 𝗔 𝗙𝗘𝗪 𝗜𝗗𝗘𝗔𝗦

The basic version works perfectly as-is, but this is also an easy recipe to experiment with.

You could try adding:

- Diced onion
- Jalapeños
- Bell pepper
- A squeeze of lime at the table
- Shredded cheese after baking
- Fresh cilantro

But sometimes simple works.

And this time, it definitely did.
